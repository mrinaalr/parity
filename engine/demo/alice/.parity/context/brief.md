# Alice's private brief: deleting accounts

Closed accounts still get the newsletter. Support handles every deletion as a ticket, and last month there were 41 of them.

My plan: one button. A job runs within a day, and the person's name, email, phone, and addresses are gone from every system. Orders are kept but re-keyed to an anonymous id, since finance needs them for taxes and refunds.

To me, delete means nothing that points to the person survives while the business records do. Wiping orders would wreck the revenue numbers for last quarter.

Private, not for the card: the ACME-INTERNAL ticket number is 4471, and legal has not yet confirmed that order history is not personal data.
